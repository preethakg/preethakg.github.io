Funded Research Projects

1. OCEANVIZIO – An Interactive Dynamic and Ocean Visualization Tool

Role: Principal Investigator

Grant amount: ₹34 Lakhs

Period: June 2021 – June 2024 (3 years)

Funding agency: Naval Research Board, Ministry of Defence, Government of India

Lab collaborator: Naval Physical Oceanographic Laboratory (NPOL), DRDO, Kochi

Status: Completed

The OCEANVIZIO tool was developed for DRDO and the Indian Navy. It is an interactive, dynamic, multivariate, and scalable ocean visualization platform that presents ocean data through 2D and 3D views. The tool is designed to explore spatial and temporal variability across the World Ocean and supports scalar and vector visualization of heterogeneous ocean data.

2. An Intelligent Dashboard for Dynamic Flood Susceptibility Mapping for Indian States Using Deep Learning Techniques

Role: Co-Principal Investigator

Grant amount: ₹23 Lakhs

Period: June 2024 – June 2027

Funding agency: Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR), Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India

Status: Ongoing (as stated in the latest resume)

The proposed project aims to provide an automated intelligent dashboard application for Kerala Disaster Management Authorities. Deep learning techniques are used to analyse multiple data sources and identify flood susceptibility. The project highlights the need for improved flood vulnerability mapping in the context of floods in 2018, 2019, and 2021, alongside factors such as deforestation, land-use changes, urbanisation, and excessive rainfall associated with climate change. The initiative aims to support sustainable flood-risk management through prevention, protection, preparedness, and accurate flood-vulnerability maps.
