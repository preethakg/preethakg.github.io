About Me

I am Dr. Preetha K.G., Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Rajagiri School of Engineering & Technology, Kerala, India. I have been associated with RSET since 2004 and have over two decades of experience in teaching, research, academic administration and institutional development.

I received my Ph.D. in Mobile Ad hoc Networks from Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) in 2018. My research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Networks, Cybersecurity and intelligent ocean data analytics.

My research focuses on developing practical AI-driven solutions for real-world challenges, particularly in ocean science, environmental monitoring, flood susceptibility assessment and cybersecurity. I am also involved in funded research projects, interdisciplinary collaborations and research mentoring.

Research Projects

OceanVizio – Intelligent Ocean Data Analytics

Principal Investigator. An AI-enabled ocean data visualization and analytics platform for the detection, analysis and visualization of ocean eddies using two-dimensional and three-dimensional representations. The project has received support from NRB, DRDO and the Indian Navy, with deployments at NPOL and Navy establishments.

Dynamic Flood Susceptibility Mapping for Indian States using Deep Learning Techniques

A CSIR-ASPIRE funded research project focused on developing intelligent flood susceptibility mapping approaches using machine learning, deep learning, rainfall, terrain, hydrological and environmental parameters.

Explainable AI for Enterprise Firewall Log Analytics

Development of an explainable AI-driven behavioural anomaly detection framework for enterprise firewall logs, incorporating device-level profiling, anomaly scoring and SHAP-based explanations.
